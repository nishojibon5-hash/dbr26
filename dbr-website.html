About Our Workshop

At DBR Complete Auto Centre in Malgram, Bogura, we handle engine and gearbox repairs, wheel alignment and balancing, denting and painting, hybrid vehicle servicing, dual-clutch systems, brake boosters, diagnostics, electrical work, professional car washing and routine maintenance. Our skilled team focuses on dependable repairs, clear advice and quality service for every customer.
